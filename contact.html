Questions

Before you book.

How much employee time does Level Set take?

Four hours. The six modules can run as a single half-day session or be split across two sessions to fit your onboarding schedule.

Who delivers the program?

Dr. Rosalind Cohen and Clinton Kelly deliver every session personally. There is no bench of facilitators; you get the two people who built the curriculum.

Is this in person or virtual?

Both. We deliver in person at your offices or virtually for distributed teams. Most organizations choose in person for the dining and presence modules where possible.

Is Level Set a compliance program?

No. We can't manage compliance for you, and we don't try. Level Set covers the other three Cs of onboarding: clarification, culture and connection. The result is confidence.

Which employees benefit most?

Early-career hires, technically gifted employees whose communication skills need to catch up to their analytical strengths, professionals without inherited corporate fluency and anyone moving into a formal corporate setting from academic, creative, nonprofit, military or entrepreneurial environments.

How is the program tailored?

Before delivery we learn your dress norms, your meeting culture and how your organization handles disagreement, and we adjust the examples so the program sounds like your company.
